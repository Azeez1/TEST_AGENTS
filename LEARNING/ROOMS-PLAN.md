You said it yourself: *"I just need to get in rooms and relationships."* This plan is only about that one move. It is NOT a new sales system — you already built those.

## Weekly Rooms Motion (light — 3 actions/week)
| Action | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Reach** | Open 1 new conversation — a DM to someone who engaged, a warm outbound touch, or 1 job application |
| **Convert** | Move 1 existing thread forward — comment → DM, DM → call, reply to inbound within 60 min |
| **Book** | Aim for the ask — a Leak Scan call, a coffee, an interview screen |

That's it. Reach → Convert → Book. One of each, weekly. The content keeps the top warm; this fills the middle.

## The mindset (from your own playbooks)
- **You're always selling** — the factory + artifacts ARE the pitch; you're not "going to get a client," you're moving warm signal into rooms.
- **Tease, don't deliver** — give a free sliver (a spotted leak) that ladders to the paid Leak Scan.
- **Whoever reaches out first has less leverage** — so lead with value/proof, not a pitch.
