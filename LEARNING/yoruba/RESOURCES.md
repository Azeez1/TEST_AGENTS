# Yoruba Resources

## Knowledge

- [Yorùbá Yé Mi: A Beginning Yorùbá Textbook - COERLL, University of Texas at Austin](https://coerll.utexas.edu/yemi/)
  Open-access multimedia beginner course with textbook PDFs, dialogues, monologues, songs, audio, tone work, and cultural context. Use as the main curriculum spine.
- [FSI Yoruba Basic Course](https://www.fsi-language-courses.org/fsi-yoruba-basic-course/)
  Large public-domain-style course archive with a student text, tone/pronunciation drills, dialogues, and extensive audio. Use especially for tone, pronunciation, and shadowing drills.
- [University of Iowa Yoruba Language and Culture Resources](https://clcl.uiowa.edu/language-resources/yoruba-language-and-culture-resources)
  Curated university resource index for dictionaries, grammar, vocabulary, listening, speaking, reading, writing, and culture. Use when a narrower supplement is needed.
- [Columbia University Language Resource Center: Yoruba](https://lrc.columbia.edu/languages/yoruba)
  University course overview showing a useful sequencing model: first-semester speaking/listening, second-semester writing/translation/grammar. Use as a benchmark for pacing.
- [ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines 2024](https://www.actfl.org/proficiency-guidelines-overview)
  External proficiency frame for what a learner can do in listening, speaking, reading, and writing in real-world situations. Use for milestone design and assessment language.
- [NCSSFL-ACTFL Can-Do Statements](https://www.actfl.org/educator-resources/ncssfl-actfl-can-do-statements)
  Learner-centered performance statements for tracking what can be done consistently over time. Use for weekly gates and self-assessment.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Local Yoruba speakers, cultural associations, Nigerian community groups, churches, mosques, or language meetups.
  Use for real pronunciation feedback, greetings etiquette, and natural phrase choice.
- Online tutors or language exchange partners who are native Yoruba speakers.
  Use for weekly conversation practice and correction once core greetings and sentence patterns are in place.

## Gaps

- Confirm the learner's goal, weekly time budget, and preferred dialect/cultural context.
- Confirm whether the learner wants formal lessons, conversational practice, reading/writing, or media comprehension as the main outcome.
- Identify a native-speaker feedback source or tutor before Phase 3.
