# Mission: Yoruba

## Why
Learn Yoruba to a professional standard, not just tourist or phrasebook ability. The working goal is to become able to use Standard Yoruba in real conversations, cultural contexts, and one chosen advanced domain without depending on English as the default escape route.

## Success looks like
- Hold a 20-minute Yoruba conversation in a chosen domain with limited English support.
- Hear and reproduce Yoruba tone, vowel, nasal vowel, and consonant contrasts accurately enough for native-speaker correction to focus on refinement rather than basic intelligibility.
- Read and write connected Yoruba paragraphs with tone marks and underdots.
- Understand slow-to-natural native audio in familiar domains and summarize the main idea.
- Build a general vocabulary base plus a specialized glossary for family, business, culture, religion, media, or literacy.
- Explain Yoruba phrases through cultural meaning, not only literal translation.

## Constraints
- Current level is still unknown.
- Assumes the serious track is 7-10 hours per week unless the learner gives a different time budget.
- Prioritizes tone, pronunciation, listening, and useful conversation before advanced grammar.
- Requires regular audio practice because Yoruba tone is meaning, not decoration.
- Requires native-speaker or tutor feedback for professional-level progress.

## Out of scope
- Advanced academic linguistics unless it improves actual performance.
- Full professional translation until literacy and domain skill are established.
- Dialect specialization until Standard Yoruba foundations are stable.
