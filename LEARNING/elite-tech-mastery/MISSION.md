# Mission: Elite Tech Mastery

## Why
EZ wants to become elite at technology without drowning in too many simultaneous topics. The practical target is to think, build, diagnose, and sell AI systems with authority by mastering the shared first-principles limits behind system design and agentic engineering.

## Success looks like
- Can design a real system from a blank whiteboard and justify each component by the limit it solves.
- Can diagnose an agentic workflow by naming the failing lever: context, model, prompt, tools, memory, routing, controls, evals, or observability.
- Can build one production-shaped AI workflow with schemas, validators, logs, review gates, retries, and a measurable business outcome.
- Can explain the architecture plainly to a technical interviewer and to a non-technical buyer.

## Constraints
- Formal sprint starts Monday, August 24, 2026 and ends Saturday, November 21, 2026.
- The user feels overwhelmed when too many tracks are open. The plan must protect focus.
- Core study energy goes to only two roots: System Design and Agentic Engineering.
- Sales, marketing, Playwright, Hermes, and the software factory are application surfaces, not new study roots.
- Teaching style: first principles first, then Feynman plain-English explanation, then teach-back.

## Out of scope
- New domains that do not directly reinforce System Design, Agentic Engineering, or the capstone workflow.
- More AI guru content unless it fills a named gap in the plan.
- Certification trivia, framework churn, or tool-specific rabbit holes before the core bars are passed cold.
