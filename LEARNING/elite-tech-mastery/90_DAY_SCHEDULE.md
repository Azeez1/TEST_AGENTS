## The Decision

You are not missing a giant list of topics. You are missing a hard operating cadence that converts knowledge into proof.

The sprint has two roots:

1. System Design: reason from machine and distributed-system limits.
2. Agentic Engineering: reason from LLM limits and production control surfaces.

Everything else is a branch:

- Software Factory: where you build proof.
- Playwright/QA: how you verify proof.
- Sales/Rooms: where you convert proof into money or interviews.
- Hermes/voice/tools: examples, not new roots.
- WISER/DBAC business frameworks: already-built solutioning layer, not a new study root.

The operating model that merges everything lives in `THREE_PILLAR_OPERATING_MODEL.md`.
The learning protocol for training that merge lives in `THREE_PILLAR_LEARNING_PROTOCOL.md`.

## Daily Operating Loop

Minimum viable day: 45 minutes.

- 10 minutes: one notes-closed rep. Name the limit, tradeoff, and plain-English explanation.
- 25 minutes: one focused concept or implementation block.
- 10 minutes: log what changed, what is still fuzzy, and the next rep.

Standard day: 90-120 minutes.

- 10 minutes: notes-closed rep.
- 35 minutes: concept encoding or scenario design.
- 45-60 minutes: build, test, document, or mock interview.
- 5 minutes: update the relevant checklist.

Weekly rhythm:

- Monday: System Design concept or scenario.
- Tuesday: Agentic Engineering concept or scenario.
- Wednesday: implementation proof.
- Thursday: integration or failure-mode drill.
- Friday: explain it out loud as an interview answer or buyer talk track.
- Friday explanation should connect both sides: WISER/DBAC for the business problem, then System Design/Agentic Engineering for the technical solution.
- Saturday: one capstone/mock block.
- Sunday: off or light review only.
