# First Evolution — 90-Day Root Focus

**Part of:** The Evolution Codex  
**Started:** September 25, 2026  
**Goal:** Choose the most broadly useful starting roots from [The 27 Roots](THE-27-ROOTS.md). This is a new whole-person progression, separate from earlier domain-specific study plans.

Assume beginner status in every root. Existing experience does not set a level in advance; demonstrated performance will reveal it. The AI partner plays the Ciel role: proposing challenges, spotting gaps, and tracking evidence.

## The nine roots for the first 90 days

1. **Root 14 — Learning & memory:** improves the rate at which every other root can grow.
2. **Root 15 — Reasoning & judgment:** improves the quality of models and decisions used in every other root.
3. **Root 19 — Psychology & self-command:** supports attention, consistency, and performance under pressure.
4. **Root 18 — Health & physical performance:** supports the energy and capacity to keep training and applying the other roots.
5. **Root 11 — Communication & media:** makes understanding clear and usable to other people.
6. **Root 13 — Relationships & social intelligence:** improves how abilities work in situations involving other people.
7. **Root 1 — Technology & engineering:** turns ideas into tools and systems that can amplify effort and shorten feedback loops.
8. **Root 2 — Sales:** turns a proposed solution into a real commitment and supplies direct feedback from people.
9. **Root 3 — Marketing:** tests and spreads ideas beyond one-to-one conversations.

These form three connected layers: an internal engine (roots 14, 15, 19, 18), a human interface (roots 11, 13), and outward leverage (roots 1, 2, 3). The added roots extend the original six; they do not replace them.

## Mission areas Azeez wants to explore now

The nine roots above are reusable multipliers. They should be trained while pursuing meaningful subjects from the rest of the map. These interests are active candidates for the first 90 days, not postponed until the nine are mastered:

- **Ventures and resources:** Root 4 — Product & design; Root 5 — Business building & ownership; Root 6 — Finance & investing; Root 7 — Economics & markets.
- **Civilizations and ideas:** Root 21 — Languages, culture & history; Root 24 — Ethics & philosophy.
- **Field competence:** Root 27 — Natural world & outdoor competence; Root 23 — Practical life skills; Root 22 — Safety, security & risk.

A mission in one of these areas can also exercise several of the nine multiplier roots. Exploration in 90 days establishes direction and evidence; it does not assign a mastery level in advance.

## Sequence of design

This file selects **roots**. [The Capability Atlas](THE-CAPABILITY-ATLAS.md) now maps the first layer of subskills under all 27 roots. Drills and evidence standards can be chosen from that map when we design missions. Nine multiplier roots do not mean nine separate courses: missions can exercise several roots together. The 27-root map remains open for new interests and challenges.

Earlier suggestions such as self-directed learning, first-principles thinking, and AI collaboration were premature subskill examples. They do not replace the 27-root hierarchy.
