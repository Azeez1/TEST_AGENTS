# The 27 Roots

**Part of:** The Evolution Codex  
**Azeez's living map of human capabilities, built with his AI partner**  
**Root map established:** September 25, 2026  
**Current stage:** 27 capability branches with a first-pass subskill map in [The Capability Atlas](THE-CAPABILITY-ATLAS.md). Levels and training missions come later.

## Purpose

Build a whole-person map of learnable abilities, inspired by the Rimuru-and-Ciel idea of acquiring and evolving skills. Azeez and the AI partner shape it together: Azeez brings lived experience and goals; the AI partner actively proposes branches and exposes possible blind spots. The Codex evolves as new abilities are discovered, trained, and combined.

## Boundary notes from the conversation

- Technology & engineering includes cybersecurity as a technical specialty. Safety, security & risk covers security more broadly, including personal, physical, operational, and cyber risk. A skill may connect to more than one branch.
- First-principles thinking belongs under Reasoning & judgment. Learning & memory covers acquiring, retaining, and transferring knowledge.
- This is a map of possible capabilities, not a commitment to train all 27 branches at once. [The Capability Atlas](THE-CAPABILITY-ATLAS.md) expands every root into subskill families without assigning levels.

## Next step

Use the atlas to explore and refine subskill families. Assign evidence-based levels and practice missions only when ready to train them.
