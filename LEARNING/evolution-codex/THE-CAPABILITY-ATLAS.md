# The Capability Atlas

**Part of:** The Evolution Codex  
**Companion:** [The 27 Roots](THE-27-ROOTS.md)  
**Version:** 1 · September 25, 2026

This is the first child layer under all 27 roots. Each bold bullet names a subskill family; the words after it show the concrete abilities inside that family. It is a map to explore, not a score, lesson sequence, or claim that every possible micro-skill has been captured. Families can be expanded into their own pages as we train them.

**How to navigate:** The roots stay in the same numbered order as The 27 Roots. The focus label shows nine multipliers, nine current interests, and nine additional roots. All 27 receive a full first pass; the labels do not indicate a skill level.

## The 27 subtrees

### 1. Technology & engineering

**Focus:** Multiplier

- **Computing foundations:** algorithms, data structures, operating systems, and computational thinking.
- **Programming:** writing code, debugging, refactoring, and using libraries and development tools.
- **Software engineering:** modular design, version control, testing, code review, and maintainability.
- **Systems and solution architecture:** requirements, component boundaries, distributed systems, tradeoffs, and integration design.
- **Cloud and infrastructure:** compute, storage, databases, identity, networking, and deployment.
- **DevOps and reliability:** CI/CD, observability, incident response, capacity, and production operations.
- **Networks and APIs:** internet protocols, API design, authentication, messaging, and service integration.
- **Data systems:** data modeling, pipelines, analytics engineering, governance, and data quality.
- **Security and privacy engineering:** threat modeling, secure development, access control, and technical compliance.
- **AI and machine learning:** model fundamentals, data preparation, evaluation, deployment, and failure analysis.
- **Agentic systems:** tool use, orchestration, memory, controls, evaluation, and human review.
- **Hardware and emerging systems:** electronics, embedded devices, robotics, and human-computer interfaces.

### 2. Sales

**Focus:** Multiplier

- **Market prospecting:** identifying buyers, researching accounts, and finding relevant openings.
- **Outreach:** writing messages, making contact, following up, and earning a conversation.
- **Discovery:** asking questions, listening for pain, and understanding the buying process.
- **Qualification:** assessing urgency, fit, budget, authority, and decision criteria.
- **Value framing:** connecting an offer to the buyer's desired outcome and cost of inaction.
- **Demonstration:** showing proof, tailoring a presentation, and handling live questions.
- **Objection handling:** separating confusion, risk, timing, and genuine lack of fit.
- **Deal progression:** making proposals, asking for commitment, and managing next steps.
- **Account development:** onboarding, retention, expansion, referrals, and relationship continuity.

### 3. Marketing

**Focus:** Multiplier

- **Customer research:** interviews, surveys, market observation, and message testing.
- **Segmentation and positioning:** choosing an audience, category, differentiation, and promise.
- **Brand strategy:** identity, trust signals, consistency, and reputation.
- **Messaging and copy:** headlines, offers, calls to action, and audience-specific language.
- **Content strategy:** editorial planning, education, storytelling, and repurposing.
- **Channel craft:** search, social, email, partnerships, events, and community channels.
- **Campaign design:** goals, creative, targeting, timing, and coordination.
- **Growth experiments:** hypotheses, tests, conversion paths, and iteration.
- **Measurement:** attribution, funnel analysis, customer insight, and learning from results.

### 4. Product & design

**Focus:** Current interest

- **Problem discovery:** observing users, mapping jobs, and validating unmet needs.
- **Product strategy:** defining the user, value proposition, scope, and success measure.
- **Requirements and prioritization:** turning needs into specifications and ordering tradeoffs.
- **User experience:** journeys, flows, usability, accessibility, and friction removal.
- **Interaction and visual design:** interfaces, information hierarchy, feedback, and design systems.
- **Prototyping:** sketches, wireframes, mockups, and working proofs.
- **Product delivery:** roadmaps, releases, cross-functional coordination, and adoption.
- **Product measurement:** usage, retention, qualitative feedback, and outcome assessment.
- **Design judgment:** simplicity, coherence, aesthetics, and knowing what to leave out.

### 5. Business building & ownership

**Focus:** Current interest

- **Opportunity assessment:** problem selection, demand evidence, and market timing.
- **Business models:** revenue streams, pricing, costs, margins, and unit economics.
- **Venture creation:** offers, early customers, minimum viable delivery, and iteration.
- **Operating model:** roles, processes, metrics, decision rights, and control systems.
- **Partnerships:** channel relationships, joint ventures, and incentive alignment.
- **Business acquisition:** target screening, diligence, financing, negotiation, and transition.
- **Ownership structures:** equity, governance, boards, and founder or investor rights.
- **Growth and scaling:** repeatable delivery, talent, systems, and expansion choices.
- **Risk and resilience:** cash runway, concentration, legal exposure, and contingency planning.
- **Exit and succession:** transferability, sale readiness, leadership continuity, and legacy.

### 6. Finance & investing

**Focus:** Current interest

- **Financial statements:** reading balance sheets, income statements, and cash-flow statements.
- **Cash management:** budgeting, forecasting, liquidity, and working capital.
- **Financial modeling:** assumptions, scenarios, sensitivity, and decision use.
- **Valuation:** cash flows, comparables, return expectations, and uncertainty.
- **Capital structure:** debt, equity, dilution, leverage, and financing terms.
- **Portfolio construction:** asset allocation, diversification, rebalancing, and risk capacity.
- **Investment analysis:** public markets, private markets, funds, and manager evaluation.
- **Real estate analysis:** income, financing, operating costs, market context, and deal structure.
- **Personal finance:** saving, credit, insurance, taxes, retirement, and long-term planning.
- **Investment governance:** due diligence, incentives, reporting, and decision records.

### 7. Economics & markets

**Focus:** Current interest

- **Microeconomics:** supply, demand, elasticity, incentives, and tradeoffs.
- **Market structure:** competition, monopoly, network effects, and barriers to entry.
- **Macroeconomics:** growth, inflation, employment, interest rates, and cycles.
- **Money and banking:** credit creation, monetary systems, and central-bank transmission.
- **Industry analysis:** value chains, profit pools, cost structures, and industry change.
- **Behavioral economics:** biases, choice architecture, and observed behavior.
- **Labor and demographics:** skills, wages, migration, and population change.
- **Global economics:** trade, exchange rates, geopolitics, and supply chains.
- **Economic evidence:** indicators, causal claims, forecasts, and data limitations.

### 8. Operations & execution

**Focus:** Additional root

- **Project delivery:** scope, milestones, dependencies, and completion.
- **Process design:** workflow mapping, standard work, and handoffs.
- **Capacity and scheduling:** throughput, queues, staffing, and bottlenecks.
- **Quality management:** standards, inspection, defect analysis, and improvement.
- **Supply and logistics:** procurement, inventory, fulfillment, and transport.
- **Performance measurement:** metrics, dashboards, reviews, and corrective action.
- **Change implementation:** rollout planning, adoption, training, and resistance.
- **Incident and continuity operations:** escalation, response, recovery, and lessons learned.
- **Continuous improvement:** root-cause analysis, experiments, and process redesign.

### 9. Strategy & competition

**Focus:** Additional root

- **Strategic diagnosis:** goals, constraints, external forces, and decisive questions.
- **Problem selection:** finding opportunities with disproportionate consequences.
- **Competitive analysis:** rivals, substitutes, entry barriers, and strategic position.
- **Game theory:** incentives, commitments, signaling, and likely countermoves.
- **Advantage design:** capabilities, differentiation, switching costs, and defensibility.
- **Scenario planning:** possible futures, triggers, and contingent responses.
- **Resource allocation:** tradeoffs, sequencing, opportunity cost, and stopping rules.
- **Portfolio strategy:** balancing bets, time horizons, and correlated risks.
- **Strategic execution:** translating choices into priorities, metrics, and adaptation.

### 10. Leadership & organizations

**Focus:** Additional root

- **Direction setting:** purpose, goals, standards, and decision principles.
- **Talent selection:** role design, hiring, reference checking, and team composition.
- **Delegation:** outcomes, authority, support, accountability, and follow-through.
- **Coaching:** observation, feedback, development plans, and skill growth.
- **Performance management:** expectations, measurement, recognition, and difficult decisions.
- **Organizational design:** structure, information flow, incentives, and decision rights.
- **Culture building:** norms, trust, rituals, and behavior under pressure.
- **Change leadership:** alignment, communication, resistance, and transition.
- **Succession and stewardship:** developing leaders, continuity, and institutional memory.

### 11. Communication & media

**Focus:** Multiplier

- **Clear writing:** structure, plain language, editing, and argument.
- **Speaking:** voice, pacing, presence, and explaining live.
- **Storytelling:** scene, tension, evidence, and memorable framing.
- **Audience adaptation:** matching detail, language, and medium to the listener.
- **Presentation design:** slides, visuals, demonstrations, and Q&A.
- **Interviewing:** question design, follow-up, and accurate representation.
- **Visual communication:** diagrams, data displays, and information hierarchy.
- **Media production:** audio, video, publishing, and platform formats.
- **Public communication:** reputation, public narrative, and responding to criticism.

### 12. Negotiation & diplomacy

**Focus:** Additional root

- **Preparation:** interests, alternatives, reservation points, and priorities.
- **Listening and inquiry:** discovering motives, constraints, and hidden variables.
- **Value creation:** tradeable terms, options, and joint gains.
- **Bargaining:** offers, concessions, anchors, and pacing.
- **Deal design:** terms, incentives, contingencies, and implementation.
- **Conflict mediation:** de-escalation, reframing, and finding workable agreements.
- **Coalition building:** stakeholder mapping, alignment, and sequencing support.
- **Cross-cultural negotiation:** norms, context, face, and communication differences.
- **Relationship preservation:** trust, fairness, boundaries, and post-deal follow-through.

### 13. Relationships & social intelligence

**Focus:** Multiplier

- **Active listening:** attention, questions, reflection, and accurate recall.
- **Perspective taking:** considering another person's goals, beliefs, and constraints.
- **Social perception:** reading context, behavior, nonverbal cues, and uncertainty.
- **Trust building:** reliability, honesty, reciprocity, and repair.
- **Boundaries:** assertiveness, consent, expectations, and saying no.
- **Relationship maintenance:** follow-up, appreciation, shared effort, and consistency.
- **Networking:** forming genuine connections and connecting others.
- **Collaboration:** shared goals, role clarity, coordination, and credit.
- **Conflict repair:** naming the issue, hearing impact, accountability, and reconciliation.

### 14. Learning & memory

**Focus:** Multiplier

- **Learning goals:** defining the capability and what successful use looks like.
- **Skill decomposition:** finding component skills and prerequisite knowledge.
- **Comprehension and encoding:** organizing new ideas, comparing examples, and making connections.
- **Retrieval:** recalling and using knowledge without looking it up.
- **Spacing and interleaving:** scheduling recall and mixing related problems.
- **Deliberate practice:** targeting weak components with informative feedback.
- **Memory techniques:** chunking, imagery, associations, and spatial mnemonics.
- **Transfer:** applying a principle to a new problem or domain.
- **Metacognition:** monitoring understanding, error patterns, and confidence.
- **Knowledge management:** capturing, linking, revisiting, and finding what matters.

### 15. Reasoning & judgment

**Focus:** Multiplier

- **Problem framing:** defining the real question, outcome, and constraints.
- **Logic and argument:** premises, inference, validity, and counterexamples.
- **First-principles thinking:** identifying fundamentals and rebuilding from them.
- **Inversion:** asking how failure would happen and avoiding those paths.
- **Second-order thinking:** following consequences beyond the first effect.
- **Systems thinking:** feedback loops, delays, interactions, and bottlenecks.
- **Causal reasoning:** distinguishing cause, correlation, mechanism, and confounding.
- **Probabilistic thinking:** uncertainty, base rates, ranges, and expected value.
- **Forecasting and calibration:** making predictions and comparing them with outcomes.
- **Decision analysis:** options, tradeoffs, reversibility, and timing.
- **Error correction:** seeking disconfirming evidence and updating a model.

### 16. Mathematics, science & research

**Focus:** Additional root

- **Mathematical foundations:** arithmetic, algebra, functions, geometry, and calculus.
- **Statistics and probability:** distributions, sampling, uncertainty, and inference.
- **Quantitative modeling:** variables, assumptions, equations, and sensitivity.
- **Scientific method:** hypotheses, measurement, controls, and replication.
- **Study and experiment design:** comparison groups, bias reduction, and valid tests.
- **Data analysis:** cleaning, visualization, estimation, and interpretation.
- **Source evaluation:** finding primary evidence and judging methods and limits.
- **Scientific literacy:** core ideas in physical, life, earth, and social sciences.
- **Research communication:** methods, evidence, uncertainty, and reproducibility.

### 17. Creativity & the arts

**Focus:** Additional root

- **Idea generation:** divergent exploration, association, and reframing.
- **Creative selection:** taste, critique, constraints, and revision.
- **Visual arts:** drawing, composition, color, and visual storytelling.
- **Music and sound:** rhythm, melody, listening, composition, and production.
- **Writing as art:** voice, narrative, poetry, and character.
- **Performance:** acting, improvisation, movement, and stage presence.
- **Craft and making:** materials, tools, fabrication, and iteration.
- **Creative practice:** portfolio building, feedback, collaboration, and finishing work.

### 18. Health & physical performance

**Focus:** Multiplier

- **Sleep and recovery:** sleep habits, rest, fatigue recognition, and recovery planning.
- **Nutrition literacy:** food quality, fueling, hydration, and reading evidence.
- **Strength:** resistance training, technique, and progression.
- **Endurance:** aerobic capacity, pacing, and conditioning.
- **Mobility and movement:** range of motion, coordination, balance, and posture.
- **Injury prevention:** load management, safe technique, and early warning signs.
- **Body awareness:** tracking exertion, energy, pain, and response to training.
- **Health literacy:** preventive care, medical information, and seeking qualified help.
- **Sustainable routines:** training consistency and adapting to life constraints.

### 19. Psychology & self-command

**Focus:** Multiplier

- **Self-awareness:** noticing emotions, motives, triggers, and recurring patterns.
- **Attention regulation:** directing focus, returning from distraction, and managing stimulation.
- **Emotional regulation:** responding to stress, frustration, fear, and disappointment.
- **Habit design:** cues, environment, repetition, and behavior tracking.
- **Motivation:** connecting action to meaning and making progress visible.
- **Impulse control:** pausing, choosing, and managing short-term temptations.
- **Resilience:** recovering from setbacks and returning to useful action.
- **Stress management:** recognizing overload and choosing recovery or support.
- **Identity and values:** clarifying commitments and acting consistently with them.
- **Self-reflection:** reviewing behavior without distortion or self-attack.

### 20. Law, policy & institutions

**Focus:** Additional root

- **Legal literacy:** sources of law, rights, duties, and jurisdiction.
- **Contracts:** terms, obligations, remedies, and practical review.
- **Business and property law:** entities, ownership, intellectual property, and transactions.
- **Regulation and compliance:** requirements, controls, monitoring, and evidence.
- **Civic institutions:** branches of government, public administration, and accountability.
- **Policy analysis:** problem definition, options, costs, implementation, and evaluation.
- **Governance:** rules, authority, oversight, and institutional incentives.
- **Dispute processes:** negotiation, mediation, litigation basics, and resolution.

### 21. Languages, culture & history

**Focus:** Current interest

- **Language acquisition:** pronunciation, vocabulary, grammar, listening, speaking, reading, and writing.
- **Cross-cultural communication:** norms, context, translation, and avoiding misreadings.
- **Historical method:** timelines, primary sources, interpretation, and conflicting accounts.
- **World history:** major eras, exchanges, empires, revolutions, and institutions.
- **Geography:** places, environments, migration, and spatial context.
- **Anthropology:** customs, kinship, social organization, and lived experience.
- **Religion and worldviews:** belief systems, practices, and historical influence.
- **Literature and media:** stories, art, language, and how cultures represent themselves.

### 22. Safety, security & risk

**Focus:** Current interest

- **Hazard recognition:** identifying threats, exposure, severity, and warning signs.
- **Personal safety:** situational awareness, boundaries, travel planning, and safe exits.
- **Emergency preparedness:** plans, supplies, contacts, and communication.
- **First aid and response:** basic response, CPR/AED training, and when to call for help.
- **Digital security:** accounts, devices, scams, privacy, and incident response.
- **Operational security:** access, procedures, physical protection, and continuity.
- **Risk analysis:** likelihood, impact, uncertainty, and mitigation choices.
- **Crisis coordination:** roles, escalation, clear messages, and recovery.
- **Safety culture:** reporting near misses, learning from incidents, and prevention.

- **Food competence:** planning, shopping, cooking, storage, and food safety.
- **Personal administration:** records, appointments, forms, and deadlines.
- **Household finance:** bill management, comparison shopping, and debt awareness.
- **Transport and navigation:** route planning, vehicle basics, and travel logistics.
- **Tools and repair:** choosing tools, troubleshooting, and knowing when to hire help.

### 24. Ethics & philosophy

**Focus:** Current interest

- **Ethical frameworks:** consequences, duties, virtues, care, and justice.
- **Applied ethics:** working through real tradeoffs in technology, business, health, and relationships.
- **Epistemology:** what counts as knowledge, evidence, and justified belief.
- **Philosophical reasoning:** concept clarification, argument, objection, and revision.
- **Political philosophy:** freedom, authority, equality, rights, and social order.
- **Philosophy of mind and science:** consciousness, explanation, models, and limits.
- **Meaning and purpose:** values, commitments, identity, and the good life.
- **Intellectual humility:** recognizing uncertainty and changing a view responsibly.
- **Ethical action:** turning principles into choices, habits, and accountability.

### 25. Teaching & education

- **Learning objectives:** defining what a learner should be able to do.
- **Curriculum design:** sequencing concepts, prerequisites, and practice.
- **Explanation:** using examples, analogy, demonstration, and plain language.
- **Questioning:** diagnosing understanding and prompting deeper thought.
- **Assessment:** checking performance with useful tasks and evidence.

### 26. Caregiving & community

- **Navigation:** maps, compass, GPS, route finding, and orientation.

## Where the roots overlap

- **Technology and security:** Root 1 builds technical controls; Root 22 assesses and responds to risk across digital, physical, personal, and organizational settings.
- **Sales, marketing, communication, and negotiation:** Root 2 works toward individual buying commitments; Root 3 reaches and tests messages in a market; Root 11 expresses ideas clearly; Root 12 structures agreements when interests differ.
- **Business, product, finance, and economics:** Root 4 shapes what people use; Root 5 builds and owns the venture; Root 6 allocates money and evaluates returns; Root 7 explains markets and incentives around them.
- **Learning, reasoning, research, and teaching:** Root 14 acquires and retains capability; Root 15 forms judgments; Root 16 produces and evaluates evidence; Root 25 helps another person learn.
- **Health, self-command, care, and outdoors:** Root 18 develops physical capacity; Root 19 regulates behavior and emotion; Root 26 supports other people; Root 27 applies knowledge in natural environments.

- [EU/OECD financial competence framework](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/financial-competence-framework-for-adults-in-the-european-union_510f133c-en.html) — personal finance, investing, and risk.
