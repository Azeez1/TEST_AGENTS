# Peptides for Fat Loss Resources

## Knowledge

- [NHGRI: Amino acids](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Amino-Acids)
  Official plain-language definition and the distinction between amino acids the body can make and essential amino acids from food. Use for foundational definitions.
- [OpenStax Biology 2e: Proteins](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-4-proteins)
  University-level open textbook showing the central carbon, amino group, carboxyl group, hydrogen, and variable side chain. Use for molecular structure.
- [NHGRI: Peptide](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Peptide)
  Plain definition: a peptide is a short chain of amino acids. Use for the molecular starting point.
- [Structural study of retatrutide receptor activity](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41421-024-00700-0)
  Primary structural research on how retatrutide engages GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors. Use for mechanism, without assuming mechanism proves outcomes.
- [Retatrutide phase 2 obesity trial](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2301972)
  Randomized human study. Use for measured weight outcomes and adverse events.
- [Retatrutide abdominal-fat MRI study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-024-03018-2)
  Randomized substudy measuring visceral and abdominal subcutaneous fat. Use for abdominal-fat claims and study-population limits.
- [Retatrutide body-composition substudy](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40609566/)
  Human DXA substudy measuring fat and lean mass. Use for muscle-preservation claims.
- [Independent analysis of three retatrutide vials](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13445994/)
  Small analytical study showing molecule identity does not ensure label-dose accuracy. Use for product-quality reasoning, not market-wide prevalence estimates.

## Gaps

- No strong retatrutide trial evidence establishes improved focus or unchanged libido in already muscular people pursuing a small cut.
