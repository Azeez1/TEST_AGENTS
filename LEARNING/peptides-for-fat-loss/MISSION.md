# Mission: Understand peptides for fat loss

## Why
Make an informed decision about peptide claims and vial quality while considering a 5–10 lb fat-loss goal, existing muscle, strength, libido, and focus.

## Success looks like
- Explain what a peptide is and why that label alone predicts no benefit.
- Separate a molecule's mechanism, human trial evidence, and the quality of a particular vial.
- Interpret retatrutide claims in light of the user's own goal and uncertainty.

## Constraints
- Teach from first principles in plain language, one concept at a time.
- Prioritize primary research and official source documents.
- The user already understands nutrition and lifting and wants this learning track focused on peptides.

## Out of scope
- Re-teaching basic diet or training advice.
