The smallest useful fact

Your body builds many molecules from small units called amino acids. Link a short run of them together and you have a peptide.

What follows from that?

The word peptide tells you how a molecule is built. It does not tell you what it does. The order and shape of its amino acids determine which biological targets it can affect.

“It’s a peptide” is like “it’s a key.” You still need to know which lock it fits and what happens when that lock turns.

Retatrutide is one specific peptide studied because it activates GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors. That mechanism is a starting hypothesis; human trials are what tell us whether it changes body fat and what unwanted effects occur. Receptor study · Human trial
