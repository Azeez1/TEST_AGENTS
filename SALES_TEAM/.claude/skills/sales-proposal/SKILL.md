# Sales Proposal

## Overview

This skill enables creation of persuasive, professional sales proposals and presentations that win deals and communicate value effectively.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. RFP Responses
Respond to formal Requests for Proposal with structured, compliant submissions.

**Components:**
- Executive summary
- Understanding of requirements
- Proposed solution and approach
- Implementation timeline
- Pricing and commercial terms
- Company qualifications and references
- Compliance matrix

**Example request:** "Create an RFP response for an enterprise CRM implementation"

### 2. Sales Proposals
Create tailored proposals for specific prospects.

**Structure:**
1. **Executive Summary:** Problem, solution, value (1 page)
2. **Customer Challenges:** Pain points you'll solve
3. **Proposed Solution:** How your product/service addresses needs
4. **Implementation Plan:** Timeline, milestones, resources
5. **Pricing:** Transparent breakdown with options
6. **Success Stories:** Relevant case studies
7. **Next Steps:** Clear call to action

**Example request:** "Build a proposal for selling marketing automation to a B2B company"

### 3. Pitch Decks
Create presentation decks for demos and meetings.

**Typical flow (10-15 slides):**
- Problem / Market opportunity
- Solution overview
- How it works (demo)
- Value proposition and ROI
- Customer success stories
- Pricing and packages
- Why us / competitive differentiation
- Next steps

**Example request:** "Create a pitch deck for our Series A fundraise"

### 4. Business Cases
Build financial justification for purchases.

**Include:**
- Current state costs and inefficiencies
- Proposed solution investment
- Expected benefits (revenue, cost savings, productivity)
- ROI calculation and payback period
- Risk mitigation

**Example request:** "Build a business case showing 18-month ROI for our platform"

### 5. Statement of Work (SOW)
Define project scope, deliverables, and terms.

**Sections:**
- Project objectives
- Scope of work and deliverables
- Timeline and milestones
- Pricing and payment terms
- Roles and responsibilities
- Acceptance criteria
- Terms and conditions

**Example request:** "Create a SOW for a 6-month consulting engagement"

## Proposal Best Practices

### Content
- **Customer-centric:** Focus on their problems and outcomes, not just features
- **Quantify value:** Use specific numbers ($X saved, Y% improvement)
- **Proof points:** Include case studies, testimonials, metrics
- **Visual:** Use charts, diagrams, screenshots
- **Scannable:** Use headers, bullets, callout boxes

### Structure
- **Executive summary first:** Decision-makers read this only
- **Progressive detail:** High-level → detailed
- **Modular:** Easy to customize sections
- **Clear pricing:** No hidden fees or confusion

### Differentiation
- **Unique value props:** What makes you different/better
- **Risk reversal:** Guarantees, trial periods, references
- **Social proof:** Customer logos, testimonials, case studies
- **Competitive positioning:** Why choose you vs alternatives

## Common Proposal Components

### Value Proposition Template
```
For [target customer]
Who [customer need/problem]
Our [product/service] is a [category]
That [key benefit and differentiation]
Unlike [competitors]
We [unique advantage]
```

### ROI Calculator Structure
```
INVESTMENT:
- Upfront costs: $X
- Annual costs: $Y

BENEFITS (Annual):
- Cost savings: $A
- Revenue increase: $B
- Productivity gains: $C
Total annual benefit: $D

ROI: (D - Y) / X = Z%
Payback period: X / (D - Y) = N months
```
