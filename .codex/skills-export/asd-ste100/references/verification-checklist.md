# Verification checklist

- Confirm the audience, format, and STE-inspired or strict-review mode.
- Match consequential claims to evidence. Flag inference and unknowns.
- Compare actor, action, sequence, condition, exception, negation, quantities,
  units, identifiers, obligation, and uncertainty with the original.
- Keep necessary caveats in the first layer. Do not turn possibility into a
  guarantee or a sample into a universal claim.
- Check short sentences, explicit references, consistent terms, defined jargon,
  and visible prerequisites. Preserve facts when resolving wording problems.
- For strict review, identify the issue and text type. Use authoritative rules,
  dictionary meanings/parts of speech, and technical terminology. Record findings
  and mark missing checks explicitly.
- For ELI5 output, load the actual skill and follow its presentation checks.
  Compare labels, controls, examples, and revealed details with the source.
- Report results and limitations accurately. Avoid unsupported compliance,
  certification, affiliation, or video-production claims.
