# About Sterling Legal Services

Sterling Legal Services is a personal injury law firm serving clients across North Carolina.

## Who We Help

We represent individuals who have been hurt because of someone else's negligence — car accidents, truck and motorcycle crashes, pedestrian accidents, slip-and-fall injuries on someone else's property, dog bites, workplace injuries, and wrongful death cases. We focus exclusively on personal injury law so every case gets the depth of attention it deserves.

## How We Work

- **Free initial consultation.** Every prospective client gets a no-cost evaluation with one of our attorneys.
- **No fee unless we win.** We work on contingency — you pay nothing up front, and we only collect if we recover money for you.
- **24/7 intake.** This AI assistant handles calls outside business hours so you never have to wait until Monday to get the process started.

## Service Area

Sterling Legal Services takes cases throughout North Carolina, with attorneys licensed in state and federal courts. We handle matters arising in any NC county.

## What Makes Us Different

- Boutique firm — we keep caseloads intentionally low so each client gets direct attorney attention, not handoffs to paralegals.
- Investigation-first approach — we send investigators to the scene, pull every record, and build the case before opening settlement talks.
- Trial-ready — many PI firms settle every case. We're prepared to take cases to verdict when that's what gets you the best outcome.

## Office Hours

Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern. The AI receptionist captures intake 24/7 — an attorney will follow up during business hours, or sooner if your situation is urgent.
