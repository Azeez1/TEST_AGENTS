# Sterling Legal Services — Practice Areas

Sterling Legal Services exclusively handles personal injury cases. Below are the types of matters we accept.

## Motor Vehicle Accidents

- Car accidents (single vehicle, multi-vehicle, rear-end, head-on, intersection)
- Truck accidents (commercial trucks, semis, delivery vehicles, FedEx/UPS/Amazon)
- Motorcycle accidents
- Pedestrian and bicycle accidents
- Rideshare accidents (Uber, Lyft)
- Uninsured / underinsured motorist claims
- Hit-and-run cases

## Premises Liability

- Slip and fall (grocery stores, restaurants, retail establishments)
- Trip and fall (uneven pavement, broken sidewalks, poor lighting)
- Inadequate security (assault, robbery on commercial property)
- Swimming pool accidents
- Stairway and railing failures
- Negligent maintenance

## Animal Attacks

- Dog bites (regardless of breed)
- Other animal attacks where an owner was negligent

## Wrongful Death

- Fatal accidents where another party was at fault
- Survival actions on behalf of family members

## Workplace Injuries (Limited)

We do not handle workers' compensation directly. However, when a workplace injury involves a third party (e.g., a contractor injured by defective equipment, or a delivery driver hit by a vehicle on the job), we can pursue a personal injury claim alongside the workers' comp case.

## What We Don't Handle

We don't handle these matters — if your case involves any of these, we'll refer you to a colleague who specializes in that area:

- Workers' compensation (pure workplace injury without third-party liability)
- Medical malpractice (we refer to specialized firms)
- Divorce, custody, or other family law matters
- Criminal defense
- Immigration
- Estate planning, probate, or wills
- Business disputes, contracts, or commercial litigation
- Real estate transactions
- Tax matters
- Bankruptcy

If you call us with one of these, the AI receptionist will politely note it and either capture your info for a referral or suggest you call a different firm.

## Geographic Scope

We accept cases anywhere in North Carolina. For incidents that happened in other states involving North Carolina residents, contact us — we may be able to co-counsel with a local firm.
