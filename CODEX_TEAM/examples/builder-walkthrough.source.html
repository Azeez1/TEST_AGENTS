    parser.add_argument("source", type=Path)
    parser.add_argument("--output", required=True, type=Path)
    parser.add_argument("--force", action="store_true")
    args = parser.parse_args()
    try:
        print(build(args.source, args.output, force=args.force))
    except (OSError, ValueError) as exc:
        parser.exit(1, f"Build failed: {exc}\n")

INFERRED INTENT

A suitcase, not a shipping service.

The builder packs local assets into one file. Its strict marker counts appear intended to catch incomplete or ambiguous source templates. That intent is an interpretation; the checks themselves are observed.

UNVERIFIED BY THE BUILDER

A successful build is not a correct lesson.

The builder does not check whether your explanation is true, whether arbitrary JavaScript works, or whether your custom HTML uses external resources. Inspect and test those separately.

Where to read next

main() → build() → test_build.py → your lesson HTML. The five bundled packaging tests were executed successfully. They cover the included cases, not every possible input.
