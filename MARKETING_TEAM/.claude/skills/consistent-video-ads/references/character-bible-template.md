# Character Bible Template

Use this template to create consistent character descriptions for multi-clip video ads. Copy the completed description EXACTLY into every clip prompt.

---

## Template

```
CHARACTER: [NAME/ROLE]

PHYSICAL:
- Age: [e.g., late 20s, early 40s]
- Hair: [color, length, style - e.g., "shoulder-length dark brown hair, natural waves"]
- Skin: [tone - e.g., "warm olive skin", "fair complexion"]
- Build: [e.g., "athletic build", "average build"]

FACE:
- Expression: [default mood - e.g., "warm genuine smile", "focused concentration"]
- Makeup: [if applicable - e.g., "natural makeup with subtle pink lip gloss"]
- Distinguishing: [glasses, beard, etc.]

CLOTHING:
- Top: [exact description - e.g., "sage green oversized sweater"]
- Bottom: [if visible - e.g., "high-waisted blue jeans"]
- Accessories: [e.g., "delicate gold necklace", "Apple Watch"]

ENVIRONMENT:
- Location: [e.g., "modern minimalist kitchen"]
- Key elements: [e.g., "white marble countertops", "stainless steel appliances"]
- Background: [e.g., "large windows, indoor plants visible"]

LIGHTING:
- Source: [e.g., "soft natural morning light from windows"]
- Quality: [e.g., "bright and airy", "warm golden tones"]
- Shadows: [e.g., "soft diffused shadows", "dramatic side lighting"]

CAMERA:
- Default shot: [e.g., "medium shot, waist-up"]
- Style: [e.g., "slight handheld movement", "static tripod"]
- Depth: [e.g., "shallow depth of field, background slightly blurred"]
```

## Usage Instructions

1. **Create your character bible** using the template above
2. **Format as a single paragraph** for the prompt:

```
A woman in her late 20s with shoulder-length dark brown hair and natural waves, warm olive skin, wearing a sage green oversized sweater. She has a warm genuine smile with natural makeup and subtle pink lip gloss. Standing in a modern minimalist kitchen with white marble countertops and large windows. Soft natural morning sunlight, bright and airy atmosphere. Medium shot with slight handheld movement.
```

3. **Copy this EXACT text** into every clip prompt
4. **Never paraphrase** - consistency comes from identical wording
5. **Add per-clip actions** after the character description

---

## Per-Clip Action Format

```
[CHARACTER BIBLE PARAGRAPH]

ACTION: [What happens in this specific clip]
CAMERA: [Any camera changes for this clip]
DURATION FOCUS: [Key moment to emphasize]
```

**Example for Clip 2 of 3:**

```
A woman in her late 20s with shoulder-length dark brown hair and natural waves, warm olive skin, wearing a sage green oversized sweater. She has a warm genuine smile with natural makeup and subtle pink lip gloss. Standing in a modern minimalist kitchen with white marble countertops and large windows. Soft natural morning sunlight, bright and airy atmosphere.

ACTION: She picks up the [PRODUCT] from the counter, examining it with genuine curiosity, then opens the package with anticipation.
CAMERA: Medium shot, slow push-in toward her hands as she opens the product.
DURATION FOCUS: The moment of opening should be center of the 8-second clip.
```
