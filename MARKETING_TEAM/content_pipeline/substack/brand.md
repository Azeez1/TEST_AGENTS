# Substack Brand — EZ

## Publication Name Options

Pick one once activated:
- **The Machina Letter** — ties to Dux Machina, feels operating-system-coded
- **Ground Truth** — Tech Samurai + McKinsey precision, says "I don't deal in noise"
- **Second Brain, First Principles** — frames the synthesis engine explicitly
- **The Compound** — compounding knowledge / returns / systems
- **The Architect's Notebook** — ties to Elite Architecture Accelerator positioning

Default pick if undecided: **Ground Truth** — terse, earned, signals the whole voice.

## Voice

Inherits LinkedIn voice guide:
- No hashtags
- No brand/company name in the essay itself (bio only)
- Long-form (1,200-1,800 words typical)
- Pierces buyer psychology
- Frameworks > motivation
- Sharp endings, not wrap-up paragraphs
- "Tech Samurai meets McKinsey Strategist"

## Positioning

**Subtitle:** "Operating-system-level thinking for the people building what comes next."

**Audience:**
- Primary: enterprise CTOs, growth-stage founders, SMB operators
- Secondary: knowledge workers trying to stay ahead of AI displacement
- Tertiary: other consultants and content creators watching the space

**What this publication is NOT:**
- AI tutorial / prompt library (too commoditized)
- Productivity porn (shallow, already saturated)
- Pure commentary / think-pieces (no action)
- Dux Machina sales material (keep the essays independent of the business)

**What this publication IS:**
- Frameworks you can run on your own work Monday morning
- Second-order thinking on topics everyone else is first-order about
- Specific, researched, grounded — not vibes
