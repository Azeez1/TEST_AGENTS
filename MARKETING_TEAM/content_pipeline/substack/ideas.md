## Topic Generator Questions (when queue runs low)

For each concept page in your wiki with 20+ backlinks, ask:

1. What does the average reader MISUNDERSTAND about this concept?
2. What does this concept predict about the next 2 years of [industry]?
3. How does this concept reveal something uncomfortable about how most people work?
4. What's the inverse of this concept, and why is it more actionable?
5. If this concept were a diagnostic test, what would pass and fail look like?

One concept page + those 5 questions = 5 essay angles.
