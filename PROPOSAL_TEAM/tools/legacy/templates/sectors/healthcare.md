## Healthcare Sector Compliance & Security

### Healthcare Privacy & Security Frameworks

Our organization maintains comprehensive compliance with healthcare privacy, security, and interoperability standards:

#### HIPAA Compliance

**Privacy Rule**:
- Protected Health Information (PHI) safeguards
- Minimum necessary standard implementation
- Patient rights management (access, amendment, accounting of disclosures)
- Privacy policies and procedures
- Workforce training and awareness

**Security Rule**:
- **Administrative Safeguards**:
  - Risk analysis and management program
  - Workforce security and training
  - Information access management
  - Security incident procedures
  - Contingency planning and disaster recovery

- **Physical Safeguards**:
  - Facility access controls
  - Workstation security policies
  - Device and media controls
  - Secure disposal procedures

- **Technical Safeguards**:
  - Access controls (unique user IDs, automatic logoff)
  - Audit controls and logging
  - Integrity controls and verification
  - Transmission security (encryption in transit)
  - Encryption at rest for PHI

**Breach Notification Rule**:
- Breach risk assessment procedures
- 60-day notification timeline compliance
- HHS breach portal reporting
- Media notification for large breaches (500+)

**Business Associate Agreements (BAA)**:
- Comprehensive BAA templates and execution
- Subcontractor BAA management
- Business associate compliance monitoring
- Chain of trust documentation

#### HITECH Act Compliance

**Enhanced Security Requirements**:
- Meaningful Use security standards
- Encryption safe harbor provisions
- Enhanced breach notification procedures
- Business associate liability compliance
- Patient right to electronic copy (within 30 days)
- Accounting of disclosures from EHRs (3 years)

**Penalty & Enforcement**:
- Increased civil and criminal penalties awareness
- Willful neglect prevention programs
- Audit response procedures
- Corrective action plan capabilities

#### HITRUST CSF Certification

**Certification Levels**:
- Specify: e1 (self-assessment), i1 (interim validated), or r2 (validated assessment)
- Annual recertification and continuous monitoring
- MyCSF assessment tool utilization
- Third-party validated assurance

**Control Framework Coverage**:
- Integration of HIPAA, NIST, ISO 27001, PCI-DSS
- 14 control categories with risk-based requirements
- Maturity model scoring and benchmarking
- Gap analysis and remediation tracking

**Benefits for Stakeholders**:
- Single framework covering multiple standards
- Recognized by payers and business associates
- Reduced audit burden through certification inheritance
- Standardized security and privacy assurance

#### Interoperability Standards

**HL7 Standards**:
- **HL7 v2.x**: Legacy messaging for lab results, ADT, orders
- **HL7 v3**: Clinical Document Architecture (CDA), structured documents
- **HL7 FHIR (R4)**: Modern RESTful API for health data exchange
  - Patient, Observation, Condition, Medication resources
  - SMART on FHIR authentication and authorization
  - Bulk Data Access (FHIR Bulk Data Export)
  - US Core Data for Interoperability (USCDI) compliance

**Meaningful Use / Promoting Interoperability**:
- EHR certification (ONC-ACB certified)
- Clinical Quality Measures (CQM) reporting
- e-Prescribing integration
- Health information exchange (HIE) participation

**Data Exchange Standards**:
- C-CDA (Consolidated Clinical Document Architecture)
- Direct Secure Messaging (Direct Protocol)
- XDS (Cross-Enterprise Document Sharing)
- NCPDP (pharmacy transactions)
- X12 (claims, eligibility, remittance)

### Healthcare Data Protection

**PHI Encryption**:
- AES-256 encryption at rest
- TLS 1.2+ for data in transit
- Encryption key management (FIPS 140-2 compliant)
- Mobile device encryption and MDM

**Access Controls**:
- Role-based access control (RBAC) aligned with patient care
- Minimum necessary access enforcement
- Emergency access procedures (break-the-glass)
- User activity monitoring and anomaly detection
- Automatic session timeout and logout

**De-identification**:
- HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification method
- Expert determination statistical disclosure control
- Limited data sets with data use agreements
- Re-identification risk analysis
