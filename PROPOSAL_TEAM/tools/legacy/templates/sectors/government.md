## Government Sector Compliance & Security

### Federal Compliance Frameworks

Our organization maintains comprehensive compliance with federal security and regulatory requirements:

#### FedRAMP (Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program)
- **Certification Level**: FedRAMP Moderate (or specify actual level)
- **NIST 800-53 Controls**: Full implementation of required control families
- **Continuous Monitoring**: 24/7 ConMon program with automated compliance dashboards
- **3PAO Assessment**: Independent third-party assessment and ongoing monitoring
- **Key Capabilities**:
  - Encryption at rest and in transit (FIPS 140-2)
  - Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all users
  - Role-based access control (RBAC)
  - Comprehensive audit logging and SIEM integration
  - Incident response procedures with federal notification protocols

#### NIST Cybersecurity Standards
- **NIST SP 800-53**: Security and privacy controls for federal information systems
- **NIST SP 800-171**: Protection of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)
- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)**: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover
- **NIST SP 800-37**: Risk Management Framework (RMF)

#### FISMA Compliance
- **Authority to Operate (ATO)**: Current ATO status and expiration date
- **Security Categorization**: FIPS 199 categorization (Low/Moderate/High)
- **Continuous Monitoring**: Annual FISMA reporting and ongoing assessment
- **Risk Management**: Documented risk assessment and POA&M tracking

#### CMMC (Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification)
For Department of Defense contracts:
- **Current Level**: Specify Level 1, 2, or 3
- **C3PAO Assessment**: Third-party certified assessment
- **CUI Protection**: Comprehensive controls for Controlled Unclassified Information
- **DFARS Compliance**: Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement adherence

#### Additional Government Standards
- **CJIS** (Criminal Justice Information Services): For law enforcement data
  - Background checks and security clearances
  - Advanced authentication and encryption (FIPS 140-2)
  - Physical security and audit trails

- **Section 508 Compliance**: Accessibility for all deliverables
  - WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance
  - Screen reader compatibility
  - Keyboard navigation and accessible forms
  - Accessible document formats (PDFs, Word, etc.)

- **ITAR/EAR**: Export control compliance for defense/aerospace
  - US persons only access restrictions
  - Controlled technical data encryption
  - Export authorization procedures
