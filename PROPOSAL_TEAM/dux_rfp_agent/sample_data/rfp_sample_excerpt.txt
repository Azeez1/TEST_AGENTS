REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
CLOUD-BASED CASE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

RFP NUMBER: GOV-2025-001
ISSUE DATE: January 15, 2025
DUE DATE: March 1, 2025

================================================================================

SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND

1.1 Purpose
The Department of Administrative Services seeks proposals from qualified vendors to provide a comprehensive cloud-based case management system to support our operations across multiple agencies.

1.2 Background
The Department currently processes approximately 50,000 cases annually across five divisions. The current legacy system is over 10 years old and lacks modern features required for efficient case management.

================================================================================

SECTION 2: SCOPE OF WORK

2.1 System Requirements

The proposed solution MUST meet the following requirements:

2.1.1 Cloud Infrastructure
- The system MUST be hosted on a FedRAMP-authorized cloud platform
- The vendor SHALL ensure 99.9% uptime during business hours (8 AM - 6 PM EST)
- The system MUST support horizontal scaling to handle peak loads
- Data centers MUST be located within the United States

2.1.2 Security and Compliance
- The system MUST comply with NIST SP 800-53 security controls
- The vendor MUST implement multi-factor authentication for all users
- All data MUST be encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
- The system SHALL support role-based access control (RBAC)
- The vendor MUST provide annual security audits and penetration testing

2.1.3 Functional Requirements
- The system MUST support case intake, assignment, tracking, and closure
- The solution SHALL provide automated workflow capabilities
- The system MUST integrate with existing Active Directory for authentication
- The platform SHOULD provide mobile access via responsive web design
- The system MUST generate customizable reports and dashboards
- The solution SHALL support document management with version control

2.1.4 Performance Requirements
- The system MUST support at least 500 concurrent users
- Page load times SHALL not exceed 3 seconds under normal load
- The system MUST process batch operations of up to 10,000 records
- Database queries SHOULD return results in under 2 seconds

2.1.5 Integration Requirements
- The system MUST provide RESTful APIs for integration
- The solution SHALL support SAML 2.0 for single sign-on
- The platform MUST integrate with our existing email system
- The vendor SHOULD provide pre-built connectors for common systems

================================================================================

SECTION 3: VENDOR QUALIFICATIONS

3.1 Experience Requirements
- The vendor MUST have at least 5 years of experience providing case management solutions
- The vendor SHALL provide at least 3 references from government clients
- The vendor MUST demonstrate experience with FedRAMP compliance
- The vendor SHOULD have ISO 27001 certification

3.2 Staffing Requirements
- The Project Manager MUST hold PMP certification
- Technical staff SHALL have relevant cloud platform certifications
- Security personnel MUST hold CISSP or equivalent certification
- All staff working on the project MUST pass background checks

================================================================================

SECTION 4: PROJECT MANAGEMENT

4.1 Implementation
- The vendor MUST provide a detailed implementation plan within 30 days of award
- The implementation MUST be completed within 9 months of contract signing
- The vendor SHALL provide weekly status reports during implementation
- The solution MUST include user acceptance testing (UAT) before go-live

4.2 Training and Documentation
- The vendor MUST provide comprehensive user training
- Training materials SHALL be provided in multiple formats
- The vendor MUST deliver complete system documentation
- Online help and knowledge base SHALL be included

4.3 Support and Maintenance
- The vendor MUST provide 24/7 technical support
- Critical issues SHALL be responded to within 1 hour
- High priority issues MUST be responded to within 4 hours
- The vendor SHALL provide regular system updates and patches

================================================================================

SECTION 5: PROPOSAL SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS

5.1 Technical Proposal
Vendors MUST submit the following:
- Executive Summary (2-3 pages)
- Technical Approach demonstrating compliance with all requirements
- Management Approach including project plan and timeline
- Staffing Plan with resumes of key personnel
- Past Performance with at least 3 similar projects

5.2 Cost Proposal
The cost proposal SHALL include:
- Implementation costs (one-time)
- Annual licensing and subscription fees
- Training costs
- Ongoing support and maintenance costs
- Any additional costs

================================================================================

SECTION 6: EVALUATION CRITERIA

Proposals will be evaluated based on:
- Technical Approach (40 points)
- Past Performance and Experience (25 points)
- Management Approach (15 points)
- Cost (20 points)

================================================================================
