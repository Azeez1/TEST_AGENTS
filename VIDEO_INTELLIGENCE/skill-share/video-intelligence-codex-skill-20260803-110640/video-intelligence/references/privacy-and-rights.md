# Privacy, Rights, and Safety

## Media authority

Analyze only media the user supplied, linked, or explicitly authorized for
research. Do not expand one URL into account-wide scraping without a separate
request. Preserve source URL, acquisition method, and available attribution.

## Private and client media

- Prefer a paid API project for private or client content.
- Set Interactions API `store=false`.
- Delete uploaded provider files after successful or failed processing.
- Keep outputs local unless the user requests external storage or delivery.
- Avoid placing raw video or extracted frames in Git-tracked folders.

## People and sensitive traits

- Do not identify an unknown person from appearance.
- Do not infer race, ethnicity, religion, health, disability, sexuality,
  political views, or other sensitive traits from video.
- Describe observable actions and source-provided roles.
- Treat emotion, intent, deception, and impairment as uncertain unless directly
  established by reliable context.

## Competitive and creative research

- Analyze patterns and evidence; do not clone protected expression.
- Keep examples rights-safe and attributable.
- Do not equate longevity, views, or engagement with verified profitability.
- Separate observed creative mechanics from inferred business performance.

## High-stakes use

Video analysis can miss fast events and hallucinate. Require human review for
legal, medical, employment, financial, safety, surveillance, or enforcement
decisions. The skill provides evidence organization, not a final adjudication.
