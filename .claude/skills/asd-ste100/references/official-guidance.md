# Official guidance and scope

Verified on 2026-10-05 against the full official web pages below. The homepage
and current overview identify Issue 9, dated January 15, 2025, as current.
Recheck when a task depends on the latest issue; a contract may require another.

- [Official STEMG homepage](https://www.asd-ste100.org/): ownership and current issue.
- [Current overview](https://www.asd-ste100.org/about_STE.html): scope, rules,
  vocabulary, and technical terminology.
- [Official FAQ](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_faq.html): general-writing adaptation,
  procedural/descriptive distinctions, and limits of tools.
- [Official downloads](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html): request an
  official copy and read the guidance on AI and verification.

ASD-STE100 is a controlled-language standard for technical documentation. It
combines writing rules with a controlled dictionary. Vocabulary checks concern
meaning and part of speech, not merely list membership. Technical nouns and
verbs also need the standard's terminology rules.

The FAQ permits applying principles such as brief sentences and active voice
in other writing contexts. This skill uses that approach by default. Progressive
disclosure, source tracking, and optional ELI5 presentation are local choices;
they do not implement the full standard.

Official guidance says tools do not replace the standard and warns that plausible
AI output is not evidence of compliance. Report strict-review coverage and
unresolved checks. Do not imply ASD or STEMG approval or certification. Respect
their ownership and rights. Link to the standard instead of copying its
dictionary or distributing a substitute standard.

Use the current overview above. The older `about.html` page still mentions
Issue 8 and is not the current-issue reference.
