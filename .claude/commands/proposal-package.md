# Proposal Package

Complete RFP response and sales proposal creation with supporting materials.

## What This Does

Orchestrates the Sales and Marketing Teams to create comprehensive proposals including:
1. RFP/RFI response analysis and strategy
2. Executive summary and value proposition
3. Technical solution and approach
4. Pricing and commercial terms
5. Case studies and social proof
6. Supporting marketing materials
7. Presentation deck

## Usage

```
/proposal-package [prospect/RFP name] [solution type] [deadline]
```

## Example

```
/proposal-package "Acme Corp CRM Implementation" "enterprise-saas" "2-week-deadline"
/proposal-package "City of Austin Smart Analytics" "government-contract" "urgent"
/proposal-package "Series A Fundraising" "investor-pitch" "next-month"
```

## Process

Use the sales-manager to coordinate this workflow:

1. **Discovery & Analysis** (sdr-agent + account-executive)
   - RFP/requirements analysis
   - Stakeholder mapping
   - Pain point identification
   - Decision criteria understanding
   - Competitive landscape assessment
   - Compliance requirements check

2. **Solution Design** (account-executive + proposal-specialist)
   - Solution architecture and approach
   - Implementation timeline and phases
   - Success metrics and KPIs
   - Risk mitigation strategies
   - Resource allocation plan
   - Value proposition framework

3. **Pricing Strategy** (sales-operations + account-executive)
   - Pricing model development
   - Commercial terms and conditions
   - ROI and business case calculations
   - Discount strategies (if applicable)
   - Payment terms and milestones
   - Contract structure recommendations

4. **Content Creation** (proposal-specialist + copywriter from Marketing)
   - Executive summary (compelling narrative)
   - Company overview and credentials
   - Technical solution details
   - Implementation methodology
   - Team bios and qualifications
   - Terms and conditions

5. **Social Proof & Evidence** (customer-success-manager + research-agent)
   - Relevant case studies
   - Customer testimonials and references
   - Success metrics and outcomes
   - Industry awards and recognition
   - Certifications and compliance badges
   - Client logos and partnerships

6. **Visual Assets** (visual-designer + presentation-designer from Marketing)
   - Professional proposal design
   - Infographics and diagrams
   - Process flow visualizations
   - Data visualizations and charts
   - Brand-compliant formatting
   - Custom graphics for solution architecture

7. **Presentation Deck** (presentation-designer + account-executive)
   - Executive presentation (15-30 slides)
   - Pitch deck for verbal presentation
   - Leave-behind materials
   - Demo script and talking points
   - Q&A preparation

8. **Review & Polish** (sales-manager + editor from Marketing)
   - Content accuracy verification
   - Brand voice compliance
   - Grammar and formatting check
   - Pricing validation
   - Legal and compliance review
   - Final quality assurance

## Deliverables

- Complete RFP response document (PDF + editable format)
- Executive summary (2-3 pages)
- Detailed technical proposal
- Pricing and commercial terms sheet
- Implementation timeline (Gantt chart or visual)
- Case studies (2-4 relevant examples)
- Executive presentation deck (PowerPoint/Google Slides)
- Supporting one-pagers and datasheets
- References and testimonials
- Contract templates (if applicable)
- Proposal submission checklist

## Time Estimate

3-5 hours for comprehensive proposal package (varies by complexity)

- `/content-suite` - For additional marketing materials

- `/customer-acquisition` - For broader sales campaign

## Notes

- For investor pitches, includes financial projections and cap table
- For government contracts, includes compliance certifications
- For enterprise sales, includes security and data privacy documentation
- All content is reviewed by editor for brand compliance (tone score 7+)
