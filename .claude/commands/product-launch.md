# Product Launch

Complete cross-team product launch coordination with engineering, marketing, and sales.

## What This Does

Orchestrates all teams to execute a comprehensive product launch including:
1. **Engineering:** Feature development, testing, deployment
2. **Marketing:** Campaign creation, content, landing pages
3. **Sales:** Proposals, pricing, collateral, training
4. **QA:** Testing, edge cases, quality assurance
5. **Finance:** Pricing strategy, revenue forecasts
6. **Operations:** Launch checklist, go-to-market plan

## Usage

```
/product-launch [product name] [launch date] [target audience]
```

## Examples

```
/product-launch "AI Analytics Dashboard v2.0" "2026-02-01" "B2B SaaS companies"
/product-launch "Premium Tier" "next-quarter" "enterprise customers"
/product-launch "Mobile App" "soft-launch" "beta users"
```

## Process

This is a **multi-phase, cross-team workflow** that coordinates all departments:

---

### **PHASE 1: PLANNING & STRATEGY** (Week 1)

#### 1.1 Product Requirements & Roadmap
**Teams:** Engineering (cto, system-architect) + Finance (cfo-agent)

- Product specification and scope
- Technical architecture review
- Resource allocation and timeline
- Budget and cost projections
- Risk assessment and mitigation

#### 1.2 Market Research & Positioning
**Teams:** Marketing (research-agent, analyst) + Sales (sales-analyst)

- Target market analysis
- Competitive landscape assessment
- Pricing strategy research
- Customer pain points and needs
- Value proposition framework

#### 1.3 Go-to-Market Strategy
**Teams:** Marketing (content-strategist) + Sales (sales-manager) + Finance (fpna-agent)

- Launch goals and success metrics
- Channel strategy (direct, partner, self-service)
- Pricing and packaging decisions
- Revenue and adoption forecasts
- Marketing budget allocation

---

### **PHASE 2: DEVELOPMENT & CONTENT** (Weeks 2-4)

#### 2.1 Feature Development
**Teams:** Engineering (cto coordinates multiple specialists)

- Feature implementation
- API development and documentation
- Frontend development (UI/UX)
- Database schema updates
- Integration and system testing
- Security and performance testing

**Use command:** `/ship-feature` for development workflow

#### 2.2 Quality Assurance
**Teams:** QA (test-orchestrator coordinates all QA agents)

- Comprehensive test suite creation
- Edge case testing
- Integration testing
- Performance and load testing
- User acceptance testing (UAT)
- Bug fixes and validation

#### 2.3 Marketing Content Creation
**Teams:** Marketing (router-agent coordinates campaign)

- Product messaging and positioning
- Website and landing page
- Blog posts and thought leadership
- Case studies and testimonials
- Product demos and videos
- Email sequences and nurture campaigns
- Social media content calendar

**Use command:** `/launch-campaign` for content creation

#### 2.4 Sales Enablement
**Teams:** Sales (sales-manager) + Marketing (copywriter, presentation-designer)

- Sales pitch deck and demo script
- Proposal templates and pricing sheets
- ROI calculator and business case
- Objection handling guide
- Competitor comparison matrix
- Customer success stories
- Sales training materials

**Use command:** `/proposal-package` for sales materials

---

### **PHASE 3: PRE-LAUNCH** (Week 5)

#### 3.1 Technical Preparation
**Teams:** Engineering (cto, devops-engineer)

- Production deployment preparation
- Infrastructure scaling and monitoring
- Rollback plan and contingencies
- Documentation finalization
- Support team training
- Analytics and tracking setup

**Use command:** `/deploy-stack` for deployment

#### 3.2 Marketing Launch Preparation
**Teams:** Marketing (social-media-manager, email-specialist, gmail-agent)

- Email sequences loaded and scheduled
- Social media posts scheduled
- Press release distribution
- Influencer and partner outreach
- Paid advertising campaigns set up
- Analytics dashboards configured

#### 3.3 Sales Preparation
**Teams:** Sales (sales-manager, sdr-agent, customer-success-manager)

- CRM updated with launch info
- Sales team training completed
- Demo environments ready
- Pricing and discounts configured
- Launch day outreach lists prepared
- Customer support FAQs ready

#### 3.4 Financial Readiness
**Teams:** Finance (cfo-agent, accountant, fpna-agent)

- Pricing systems configured
- Payment processing tested
- Invoicing templates ready
- Revenue tracking dashboards
- Financial reporting prepared
- Budget vs. actual tracking

---

### **PHASE 4: LAUNCH DAY** (Day 0)

#### 4.1 Technical Launch
**Teams:** Engineering (devops-engineer) + QA (test-orchestrator)

- Production deployment executed
- Feature flags enabled (gradual rollout)
- Real-time monitoring active
- Support team on standby
- Performance metrics tracking
- Immediate bug triage process

#### 4.2 Marketing Launch
**Teams:** Marketing (router-agent coordinates all)

- Website updates go live
- Email campaigns sent
- Social media blitz executed
- Press release distributed
- Paid ads launched
- Community announcements posted

#### 4.3 Sales Launch
**Teams:** Sales (sales-manager, sdr-agent, account-executive)

- Customer outreach initiated
- Demo requests handled
- Pricing and proposals sent
- Existing customer upsells
- Partner notifications sent
- Customer success check-ins

---

### **PHASE 5: POST-LAUNCH** (Weeks 6-8)

#### 5.1 Performance Monitoring
**Teams:** Engineering (backend-architect) + Marketing (analyst) + Sales (sales-analyst)

- Technical performance metrics
- User adoption and engagement
- Marketing campaign performance
- Sales pipeline and conversion rates
- Customer feedback collection
- Bug reports and issues tracking

#### 5.2 Optimization & Iteration
**Teams:** All teams

- A/B testing of messaging and features
- Conversion rate optimization
- Feature refinements based on feedback
- Content updates and additions
- Sales process improvements
- Pricing adjustments (if needed)

#### 5.3 Financial Review
**Teams:** Finance (cfo-agent, financial-analyst)

- Revenue vs. forecast analysis
- Cost analysis and ROI calculation
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Lifetime value (LTV) tracking
- Budget variance analysis
- Financial projections update

#### 5.4 Launch Retrospective
**Teams:** All teams + Supervisor

- What went well analysis
- What could be improved
- Lessons learned documentation
- Process improvements for next launch
- Team feedback and recognition
- Knowledge base updates

---

## Time Estimate

- **Fast-track launch:** 2-3 weeks (minimal scope)
- **Standard launch:** 4-6 weeks (recommended)
- **Major launch:** 8-12 weeks (enterprise/flagship product)

Total agent coordination time: 10-20 hours (varies by complexity)

## Related Commands

- `/ship-feature` - Engineering development workflow
- `/launch-campaign` - Marketing campaign creation
- `/proposal-package` - Sales materials creation
- `/deploy-stack` - Infrastructure deployment
- `/financial-analysis` - Pricing and revenue analysis
- `/quarterly-planning` - Strategic planning across teams

## Dependencies

- All teams must be aligned on launch date
- Engineering features must be code-complete 1 week before launch
- Marketing content should be ready 3-5 days before launch
- Sales team should be trained 1 week before launch
- QA sign-off required before production deployment

## Risk Mitigation

- **Technical risks:** Rollback plan, feature flags, gradual rollout
- **Marketing risks:** Pre-launch buzz, contingency content, A/B testing
- **Sales risks:** Training, objection handling, pricing flexibility
- **Financial risks:** Conservative forecasts, budget buffers, monitoring

## Notes

- This is the most comprehensive command in the workspace
- Requires coordination across ALL 8 teams
- Supervisor verification recommended at each phase
- Can be run in phases (use specific phase commands if preferred)
- Adjust timeline based on product complexity
- Consider soft launch or beta before full launch
